About me

Hi, I’m Brooklyn!

I’m an engineering student who loves blending design and engineering to create products that truly work for people. Whether it’s designing accessible robots in Japan or improving app experiences for kids and coaches at Hudl, I put empathy at the heart of everything I build.

Outside of class, you’ll find me 3D printing and painting mini-figures at the makerspace, volunteering to help first-generation students navigate college, or soaking up the sun at the beach :)
